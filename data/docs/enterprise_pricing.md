# Enterprise Pricing and Discount Rules

Enterprise list price is $1,200 per seat per year, with a minimum of 50 seats.

Sales representatives may offer up to 15% discount on their own authority. Discounts between 15% and 25% require approval from the Regional Sales Director. Discounts above 25% require VP of Sales approval and are granted only for multi-year contracts.
