# Salary Bands (Confidential)

Software Engineer: $70,000 - $95,000.

Senior Software Engineer: $95,000 - $130,000.

Staff Engineer: $130,000 - $170,000.

Band changes are reviewed once a year in July and must be approved by the Head of HR.
