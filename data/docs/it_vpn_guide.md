# VPN Setup Guide

Install the Acme VPN client from the IT portal. Log in with your company email and approve the multi-factor prompt on your phone.

If the VPN disconnects repeatedly, switch the protocol from UDP to TCP in the client settings. If the problem continues, open an IT ticket with the label "vpn".

Passwords must be reset every 90 days. Reset them at the identity portal, never by email.
