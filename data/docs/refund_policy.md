# Refund Policy (Acme Corp)

Standard plan customers may request a full refund within 14 days of purchase.

Enterprise customers on annual plans may request a full refund within 30 days of the invoice date. After 30 days, refunds are pro-rated based on the unused portion of the contract term.

Any refund above $10,000 requires written approval from the Finance team before it is processed. Support agents must not promise a refund to a customer before approval is granted.
